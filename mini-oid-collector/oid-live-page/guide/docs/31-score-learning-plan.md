**Status: planned. Nothing here is built yet beyond this page.** Research and paper only. Nothing new is paid for. The raw scores stay on screen at all times.

## What we already have (checked on the mini, 10/20 evening)
- **Every score change is already a feature snapshot.** From 10/21, each row in `~/oid-data/score/changes-YYYYMMDD.jsonl` (and `changes-wide-*`) stores the score, raw value, coverage, the settings version, all six inputs (`comps`: each input's c, q, w and raw measures) and the context at that moment (`ctx`: C/P 15 s/1 m, flow lean, rvol, surge, walls and wall room, momentum state, 60 s/120 s tape, market). `episodes-*` stores each hold with its gain, peak (best move) and trough (worst move).
- **Prices to grade against:** `volume_5s` (5-second bars) and `bars_1m/5m/15m/1h/1d` in `~/oid-data/archive` for the core names (JSONL kept 90 days, Parquet kept forever, 1.1 GB today) and in `~/oid-data/archive-wide` for the sector names (raw kept 21 days, 65 MB today).
- **Side inputs:** C/P history (`~/oid-optflow/data/cp-history`, 7 MB), walls history (`~/oid-data/walls-history`, from 10/20 only), `momentum_state` (from 10/20), news headlines and finbert sentiment (`~/oid-desk/state/news/sentiment-*.jsonl`, from 10/20).
- **Graders:** `score_replay_changes.py` / `score_replay_wide.py` (re-run a day at 5 s), `daily_scorecard.py` (score alone and × surge, C/P, wall room, momentum, tape, market, held-before-acting), `score_outcomes.py` (old 15-minute rows at +5 m to the close).
- **Gaps:** snapshots exist only when the score changes, so there is no fixed time grid; the 10/20 sector replay had no regular-hours 5-second bars, so it graded on 1-minute bars, and sector raw bars are kept only 21 days; sector C/P history is empty; walls history is one day; news is not joined to scores; there is no single list of what each input means; no table of outcome labels; no record of experiments.

## The plan
1. **Feature registry.** One file, `~/oid-data/config/feature-registry.json`. Each input gets one entry: name, source, unit, how often it updates, how stale it may get, which score versions use it, the date logging started, and its status (shadow, live or retired). The Field Guide reads it, so the docs and the code cannot drift apart.
2. **As-of feature table.** Keep the change rows as they are. Add a **fixed 15-second snapshot** per ticker (core and sector) with the same `comps` and `ctx` fields, plus news tone, written by the score jobs to a new archive table `score_features`. Every value carries its own age, so nothing ever looks ahead. About (65 + 144) names × 1,560 steps a day ≈ 330k rows a day: roughly 300 MB a day as flat JSONL and 30–40 MB as Parquet.
3. **Outcome labels.** A nightly job writes `score_labels`: for every snapshot and change, at **15 s, 30 s, 1 m, 2 m, 5 m, 15 m and 30 m**, the move in the score's direction, the best move (max favorable) and the worst move (max adverse) in bp and in ATR, whether the direction was right, and whether price touched the call or put wall. It uses 5-second bars and falls back to 1-minute bars, with a flag saying which. Later and optional: the outcome of a near-the-money option from the OPRA marks.
4. **Versions, experiment log and replay.** Each settings change gets a new `settings_version`; old versions are never edited. Every test is appended to `~/oid-data/score/experiments.jsonl` (date, question, versions compared, days, result, verdict). Replay gains an option to **drop one input** (or to add one), so each input's value is measured on the same days.
5. **Model ladder.** (a) Today's hand weights. (b) A calibrated logistic regression on the registry inputs. (c) Small gradient-boosted trees. Each model is trained walk-forward by day: train on days before D, test on day D, never mix days. A challenger runs in **shadow** (logged and shown as a separate column, never replacing anything). It is promoted only if it beats the current score out of sample on at least **10 trading days** by a set margin (for example +3 points of direction hit rate at 5 m with peak/trough no worse), and Jeffrey approves. The raw hand-weight score always stays visible.
6. **Weekly retrain and dashboard.** Every Saturday: rebuild the labels, retrain the challengers, write `scorecard-model-YYYY-WW.md/json`, and add a **Learning** panel to the Field Guide and system page: hit rate by horizon for the current score and each challenger, calibration (said 60% → right 60%?), each input's drop-one effect, and days left until a promotion test can be decided.
7. **Adding a new data source.** (i) Register it (status shadow). (ii) Log it into `score_features` for at least **10 trading days** without using it in any score. (iii) Run an add-one replay and a model check with and without it. (iv) Promote it only if it helps out of sample; otherwise keep it logged or retire it. News sentiment, sector C/P and Alpaca open-interest walls are the first candidates.
8. **Storage and compute (M4 mini, 16 GB, 411 GB free).** Features plus labels come to about 50 MB a day in Parquet, about 12 GB a year. JSONL (about 300 MB a day) is kept 90 days, about 27 GB at most; Parquet is kept forever (the existing `oid_archive` jobs). The nightly label job and the weekly retrain take minutes on the CPU (logistic regression and small trees, scikit-learn / LightGBM, Nice 10, after the close). No GPU and no cloud are needed.
9. **First build steps (none needs anything paid).**
   1. Feature registry plus a Field Guide page generated from it (about 0.5 day).
   2. `score_features` 15 s snapshots for core and sector names, wired into `oid_archive` (about 1 day).
   3. Nightly `score_labels` job (15 s to 30 m, best and worst move, direction hit, wall touch) plus a backfill for 10/19–10/20 (about 1 day).
   4. `experiments.jsonl`, drop-one/add-one replay, and the weekly scorecard (about 1 day).
   5. A shadow logistic-regression challenger as its own table column, walk-forward, unpromoted (about 1 day; useful after about 10 days of data).

Steps 1–4 can start now. Step 5 needs about two weeks of logged data before any result means anything.

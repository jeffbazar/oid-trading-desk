# 5 · Price and % change
[← Index](README.md)

- **Last price** (top-right of row 2): the latest SIP trade price, updated every second. After hours it shows the official close with a small **cl**.
- **% change**: `last ÷ yesterday's close − 1`. Green ▲ when up, red ▼ when down.

**Example:** `▲ +3.23%   906.92` means MU last traded at 906.92, which is 3.23% above Friday's close.

Why it's on the tile: rvol tells you *how busy* a name is, and % change tells you *which way it has already moved*. Busy plus a big move has a different meaning from busy and flat (see [walkthroughs](18-walkthroughs.md)).

The [Table](13-table.md) also shows **Day high** and **Day low**. A vol_surge often fires when price breaks one of them.

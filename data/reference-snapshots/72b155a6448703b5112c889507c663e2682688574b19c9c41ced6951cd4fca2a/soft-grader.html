Soft Grader V1 · soft second opinion

Never elevates alone · research / paper only · schema 1.0 · Built 2026-10-04 5:26 PM PT · feed as of 2026-09-28 6:27 AM PT
What it is: a cheap typed second opinion on elevate packets / take-or-skip (regime · direction · setup_quality · risk_state · toxic_flow · wrongAbout) — Jev-style lean from a compact snapshot (~400 tokens).
What it is not: a wall / FA / soft-catalyst replacement; a seat or premium authority; a Close Playbook override; an auto-elevate. Hard gates always win. Zero RH execution.

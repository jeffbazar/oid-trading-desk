# Supporting material for `docs/SCALING-STRATEGY-20261021.md`

Produced 2026-10-21 by a multi-agent review of both repositories and the three live-page screenshots dated 2026-10-20. Research and paper only. Read the main report first; these files are the evidence trail.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `map-desk-ops.md` | How the desk operates today: bot roster, the entry pipeline as actually practiced, exit rules, the caching contract and quotas, what is documented-only versus built, the decisions still made by hand, and the accounting gaps. |
| `map-research.md` | The research program as designed and as built on the research mini, the data families A to J, the R1 to R15 archive additions, studies S1 to S6, and what the 10/20 live studies do and do not show. |
| `map-ui.md` | Inventory and critique of the live page and the published review pages, with ranked redesign proposals and the decision-capture design. |
| `vendor-facts.md` | Broker, data-vendor, regulatory and open-source facts as of 2026-10-21, with the corrections from the fact-check at the top. |
| `proposals/` | Five independent proposals written from different lenses (systematic PM, execution and risk, data engineering, quant research, skeptic plus product). |
| `judge-summary.md` | Three judges' scores, rankings, must-keep ideas and the disagreements the synthesis had to settle. |
| `synthesis-draft.md` | The merged plan before fact-check corrections. Superseded by the main report where they differ. |
| `verification-log.md` | Twelve claims, two adversarial checkers each, with evidence, corrections and sources. |
| `model-plan-sources.md` | The eight claims checked for `../MODEL-AND-HARDWARE-PLAN-20261021.md` (the per-bot model and hardware plan), with verdicts, corrections and sources. |
| `model-plan/` | The three research sheets (open-weight models, Apple-silicon serving, proprietary APIs), the two draft plans (API-first, local-first) and the synthesis the model plan was built from. Superseded by the plan where they differ. |
| `mockups/` | The three UI mockups from section 9 of the main report as Claude Design artboards (`Main`, `Ticket`, `Scorecard`) plus the canvas layout. See `mockups/README.md`. |

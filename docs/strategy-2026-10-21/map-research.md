# OID Research Capability Map (as of 2026-10-21; repo `jeffbazar/oid-research`, single commit `b29a1bc` 2026-10-11)

Legend, as the repos use it: **DOCUMENTED** (in a doc), **CONFIGURED** (code/config present), **OBSERVED** (verified by a run/log), **NOT BUILT**.

## (a) The research loop as designed — `program.md` v1.0.0 (sha256 `3babdc06…770b`, 52,803 bytes, 28 sections, dated 2026-10-10)

**Modes (§2).** `DRAFT_ONLY` (inventory, propose, no runs/purchases) → `HISTORICAL_RESEARCH` (bounded campaigns on registered data in the sandbox) → `SHADOW_PAPER` (frozen model emits experimental forecasts; production ledger untouched) → `RELEASE_REVIEW` (frozen evidence package; grants no deployment or trade authority). Research Bot has no order, purchase, deploy, schedule or policy-mutation authority. "Research is a shared evaluation service, not an extra trading vote."

**Profiles (§4).** `ROSE_INTRADAY` (minutes–hours; 30-min forward outcome is an *example*), `ROSE_SHORT_SWING` (1–3 sessions), `HAL_SWING` (5–30 sessions), `HAL_CATALYST` (dated event, registered realization window), `HAL_TACTICAL_EXCEPTION`. DTE comparisons proposed (not settings): 7–21 Rose intraday, 14–45 Rose swing, 90–180 Hal. 0/1-DTE excluded unless explicitly permitted with an execution archive. The owner's seconds-to-hours, same-day style maps to `ROSE_INTRADAY` but the spec never registers a sub-30-minute horizon.

**Register before search (§8).** Immutable study spec with hashes of study, data manifest, evaluator, labels, splits, costs, risk policy. Thresholds and baselines set *before* results; a renamed study does not restore a consumed holdout.

**Budget (§12).** One shared atomic budget: **50 configs / 6 hypothesis families / 4 wall-clock hours / 2 workers / stop after 10 non-improving trials**, counted across folds, workers, retries, studies. Every subset, threshold, horizon, seed is a config; a retry gets an attempt ID under the same trial. "Stop if it cannot be measured/enforced."

**Nested walk-forward (§13–14).** All tickers split jointly by calendar; final ~20% of eligible dates sealed before research; ≥3 chronological outer blocks; per fold: expose prefix → bounded inner search → freeze → score once on the next block → advance. Outer scores withheld until all folds complete. Purge by *actual* `label_end_at`, registered embargo, time/event-block bootstrap; "many AI stocks responding to one release are not independent samples."

**Sealed holdout (§15).** One finalist per campaign, run once by a separately controlled evaluator. Once a period's results are visible to an agent or human, that period is consumed (development history). Without access separation: `INSUFFICIENT_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE`.

**Two outcome tracks (§17).** `UNDERLYING_FORECAST` (return/relative return/target-before-invalidation, Brier/log loss) and `EXECUTABLE_OPTION_STRATEGY` (net realized cashflows for a fixed contract/entry/exit policy). Without options history publish `UNDERLYING_ONLY`; theoretical repricing is `SCENARIO_ONLY`. "Do not output a trader-facing call-profit probability from a stock-direction model." Fills (§18): buy at eligible ask after decision + registered latency, sell at bid after exit + latency, size-capped, no fill inside the triggering bar.

**Lifecycle and gates (§20).** `DRAFT → REGISTERED → DEVELOPING → FROZEN → INDEPENDENT_EVALUATION → PAPER_CANDIDATE → PAPER_VALIDATED → RELEASE_REVIEW → AUTHORIZED_HANDOFF` (+ `SUSPENDED/RETRACTED/ARCHIVED`). Verdicts: `INVALID, INSUFFICIENT_DATA, NO_INCREMENTAL_VALUE, EXPLORATORY, SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS`. Historical gates: integrity, predeclared objective, measured incremental benefit, uncertainty/selection addressed, ablation/forward evidence, concentration and execution stress disclosed, independent final evaluation. Prospective paper gates: frozen version on future data with contemporaneous logs, label maturity, preregistered precision/economic criteria. `PAPER_VALIDATED` grants no live authority; `research_signal.trade_authorization=NONE` always (§22).

**Clocks (§6).** Twelve named timestamps (`source_observed_at … label_available_at, feature_computed_at`) plus replay clocks; `availability_basis` ∈ {`OBSERVED_DESK_REPLAY`, `RECONSTRUCTED_PROVIDER_REPLAY`}; `latency_model_ref` required.

## (e) What the 10/20 live studies imply about the scores

All figures from the Research tab (`studies-index.json`); scripts named: `score_replay_wide.py`, `score_replay_changes.py`, `daily_scorecard.py`, `score_learn.py`.

- **Direction score first look**: 1,610 live rows, 61 tickers, config `v1-20261019`; nonzero hit 34% at 5 m, 32% at 1 h, 27.5% at close; **corr(raw score, close return) = −0.39**. One session of correlated names ≈ one or two effective observations of market direction; this is as consistent with "the score chased intraday momentum that reversed on 10/20" as with an inverted signal. DS1 verdict `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` is the right call; flipping the sign would be hindsight.
- **score_labels (10/19–10/20 replays, 15 s snapshots)**: core |S|=2 53.5% at 5 m / 55.7% at 15 m (n=14,703 snapshots), |S|=1 47.9%; light |S|=3 59.2% / 64.8% (n=20,130), |S|=1–2 ≈45–46%; calibration core +2.0 raw → 58.0% at 5 m, **−2.5 raw → 33.0%**. 15 s snapshots are ~20× overlapping at 5 m, so effective n is far smaller; the +/− asymmetry (bearish extremes wrong 67%) looks like an up-day base rate, not skill. No same-window base rate (B0) is reported anywhere.
- **Light replay 10/20 (15,090 holds)**: ±3 fired 683×, 43% right at once; held 5 min 54.3% (n=219); **±3 + volume surge held 5 min 67.5% (n=40)**; all nonzero 38.8%. n=40 gives a binomial 95% CI of roughly 51–81%, clustered in time.
- **Core waiting (13,094 holds, median hold 40 s)**: all nonzero 38%; ±2 37% → 43.2% after 30 s (n=782); **±2 + surge held 2 min 61.5% (n=26)**; ±3 34.8% → 22.2% at 30 s (n=27). The most extreme reading performs worst: score saturates after the move.
- **C/P agreement**: agreed 41.8% (n=4,210), disagreed 46% (n=845), missing 30.6%. Not supported.
- **Score × momentum**: DECEL 46.7% (n=2,131), Quiet 34.8% (5,554), RESUME 39.6% (260), ACCEL 31.3% (163). Right most often when decelerating, worst when accelerating: consistent with chasing.
- **Wall magnet** (82,190 ticker-minutes, 325 ticker-days, 1,045 snapshots): above call wall touched within 5 min 46.9% vs 44.9% chance; above put wall 9.2% vs 9.9%; no limbo. **1-min rvol ≥ 3× triples 15-min breakout odds away from walls: 8.7% → 26.7%.** Walls ≈ no information; volume is the switch.
- **Drop-one C/P**: score changes 8,108 → 5,578 (−31%); ±2 right after 5 m **50.3% → 55.8%** (n 1,552 → 1,034); ±3 54.3% → 42.9% (n 46 → 14); all nonzero 47.6% → 49.2%. One day; the 15 s/1 m C/P input (small classified coverage) looks like churn.
- **Add-one news**: ±2 50.4% vs 50.3%; 243 fewer changes. No evidence.
- **FA quota**: 2,122/2,500 by 4:32 PM PT (VIX ~700/33%, oil 450/21%, walls 325/15%, agents ~650/31%); full wall day ~2,850; free savings 1,300–1,500.

**Net:** every "all nonzero" number is below 50% (34%, 38%, 38.8%, 41.8%, 47.6%). Conditions above 50% with n ≥ 1,000: ±2 held ≥ 5 m without flow (55.8%), |S|=2 at 15 m (55.7%, heavily overlapping). The headline numbers (67.5%, 61.5%) have n = 40 and 26. The one conditioner present in every positive cell is **1-min rvol ≥ 3×**. Under `program.md` §13–16 this is hypothesis generation, not evidence: no registration before search, no base-rate baseline, hit rate instead of Brier vs baseline, overlapping snapshots counted as n, 1–2 correlated sessions, and the researcher saw everything — so 10/19–10/20 are now consumed development dates for any study on them (§15).

## (f) Biggest blockers to making research actionable for a trading bot

1. **Independence.** The box cannot hide a holdout from agents; the research mini can but holds no data and has no network path; the LLM researcher knows 2024–2026 outcomes; every historical sealed window is contaminated. The repo's own answer: sealed holdout = **future dates ≥ D** on the research mini, which requires the network path, the archive-pull, and the root-run seal copy (all NOT BUILT).
2. **Options history (family C ABSENT).** The owner's edge is a +10–20% move *on the option*; no study can label that. Alpaca has bars+trades (Feb 2024+), no historical quotes; FA Alpha $1,499/mo; Massive $199 non-pro; Databento targeted pulls cheap but unmeasured. Forward R1/R2 capture is the $0 path and starts the clock only when enabled.
3. **PIT archive and clocks.** As of 10/10 every feed overwrote `latest/` with a single `as_of`; the mini package had "no archive, append, or snapshot writer." The 10/20 page shows archiving now exists for the page's scorer and walls, but it is page-local, not in git, not hashed into a data manifest, and not reachable by the sandbox. None of R5/R6/R1/R3/R7 has a build log.
4. **Labeling.** Page labels are "direction right at 5/15 m"; the spec needs a registered outcome, a B0 base rate, Brier/log loss, and purge by `label_end_at`. The owner's actual exit rule (+10–20% target, +50% MFE arms a trail, close at ≤ 50% of peak) has never been encoded as a label. No structured candidate/rejection log (R7) for Rose/Hal; the paper ledger mixes 6 administrative closes into 57 and its Rose result is top-3-concentrated (89% of net), which fails the §16 top-contributor diagnostic. The owner's live trades are in no repo.
5. **Latency model.** `latency_model_ref` is `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` in every study; the collector `LagHistogram` is not persisted (R15); the LLM-agent decision path on the box is seconds-to-minutes while the target horizon is seconds-to-hours. A seconds-horizon exit rule is the most latency-sensitive study the desk could run and has no measured delay.
6. **Enforcement and authorization.** No runner, no evaluator, no thresholds, `DRAFT_ONLY`; §12 forbids starting without enforcement. Campaign 1 has zero headroom (50/50 configs, 6/6 families): every page-generated idea is a campaign-2 item needing new untouched dates.

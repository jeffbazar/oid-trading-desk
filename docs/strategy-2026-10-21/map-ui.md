## (b) Usability critique for a seconds-scale decision

**Scanning cost.** 209 tiles × ~12 datums ≈ 2,500 glyphs at 6–7 px; 16 variable-width groups wrap into two rows so a name's position shifts as groups fold. The largest glyph on every tile is 1-min rvol, which reads `0.0×` on ~200 of 209 tiles in the screenshot; the second-largest is the score chip, which the page's own Research tab rates near coin-flip (all nonzero 38% at once; |S|=2 53.5% at 5 m). The one robust conditioner the studies found (1-min rvol ≥3× "roughly triples breakout odds") is encoded as pulsing, red bars and a `0 hot` counter — weaker than the score chip. Visual weight is inversely proportional to evidence.

**Encoding ambiguities.** Green means call wall, positive score, call lean, up-day and `INLINE`; red means put wall, negative score, put lean, down-day *and* a ≥3× volume bar (the "good" signal). `—` carries three meanings (no data, flat trend, closed). `C/P 1.8` (15 s/1 m Alpaca ratio), `15m CALL` (UW lean, italic = "slow"), `5m —` (UW 5 m), `day …` (day C/P) are four windows from two vendors in near-identical pill styles; italic is the only cue. Hollow vs filled 4 px dots encode wall confidence; `N-1`/`N0`, `quiet ½16¢ $1:76¢`, `ᵗᵗ` and `hot` are undefined on-screen. Coverage % ("small %") is absent from every tile in the screenshot, so a `C/P 2.5` on 3% classified coverage looks identical to one on 60%.

**Staleness.** Strong where done: dashed STALE tiles with ISO as-of, `26s ago` on futures, `detector 9h old`, group-bar `3m ago`. Weak elsewhere: no age on score chips, wall marks ("~10 min" is a legend claim, not a per-tile stamp), momentum state, or `C/P` chips; five clock formats coexist (`2026-10-20 close`, `05:00:00 PM PT`, `26s ago`, `9h old`, `1:00 PM PT`); the header chip stays green "updated 10:07:25 PM PT" while 7 of 22 columns are blank — "page updated" is conflated with "data fresh" (the same conflation the audit flagged on here.now: "A page reload or SSE heartbeat is not market liveness").

**Alert fatigue.** Five surge chips all stamped `test`; 12 momentum chips in 120 s including AMZN `accelerate` 12:58:00 → `decelerate` 12:59:00 and NFLX `accelerate` → `decelerate` 113 s later (no hysteresis); 770 news items/day sorted by likes with visibly wrong finbert signs; three disclaimer phrases (`shadow, research only`, `soft only`, `dry-run (test)`) occupy the top ~150 px. Nothing is ranked, acknowledged, deduplicated or dismissible.

**Hard to find.** Wall distance in % exists only in the Table's Walls text; "why is NVDA +2" exists nowhere on the page (the score-change log stores inputs per Research row 1, but no UI surfaces them); held-score duration (the studies' 30 s–5 m conditioner) is not shown; C/P coverage % hidden; sector scores (F±n/L±n) live only in the Table.

**Mobile / accessibility.** Live page responsiveness is NOT OBSERVED (Tailscale-only); at 209 tiles with 6 px type it cannot be phone-usable without a different mode. Green/red dot pairs on the rail and green/red score chips have no shape redundancy → fail for red-green CVD; grey-on-light `quiet` chips and 6 px numerals fail contrast/size. The local review UI is the only surface with CONFIGURED breakpoints and reduced-motion support.

**Table.** 22 columns, 7 empty after close, two score columns empty for all 65 core rows; `INLINE 1.08×` is the heaviest pill and repeats on every baseline row; no sticky symbol column; no column grouping.

**Research tab.** Readable, honest verdict chips, but n and % are buried mid-sentence; no calibration or hit-rate-by-bucket visual; no "today vs 10-day" trend; the studies the page cites (`score_replay_changes.py`, `daily_scorecard.py`) are not in the repos.

## (d) Redesign proposals, ranked (impact / effort)

1. **Re-rank the tile hierarchy** (high / low): big glyph = 1-min rvol *only when ≥1.5×*, otherwise % change; score chip demoted to a small corner mark with a **held-for timer** (`+2 · 48s`); after close, switch to a session-summary tile (day vs norm, close). Remove `0.0×` from closed tiles.
2. **One freshness grammar** (high / low): every datum gets an age pill in one format (`12s`, `4m`, `9h`) colored against *its own* TTL; header chip goes amber when any gated feed is past TTL; fix the `()` template bug; unify Last-trade clocks.
3. **Alert triage lane** (high / medium): a single "Now" lane of ≤5 ranked candidates (rank = rvol × held-score × wall proximity); hysteresis ≥60 s on momentum state flips; `test`/`shadow` chips collapsed into a muted lane; Ack / Ignore / Acted buttons that write to the decision log (see e).
4. **Ticker focus panel** (high / medium): click/tap → right drawer with the why-this-fired breakdown (each score input × weight, C/P ratio **with coverage %**, wall distances in %, UW lean + age, news tone + source), 5 s bars for the last 5 min, and the three study-backed conditioners as checkboxes (rvol ≥3×, held ≥30 s, DECEL state).
5. **Decision ticket (paper first)** (high / medium): pre-filled from the focus panel: Call / Put / Pass, contract, debit vs $5k ceiling, bracket (+10%/+20% target, stop, time stop), seat count vs 8/15; writes a structured decision row keyed to the 15 s `score_features` snapshot id. No orders until policy changes; the same ticket becomes the bot-facing schema later.
6. **Color and shape separation** (medium / low): volume = amber→red ramp only; direction/score = diverging blue/orange or green/red *plus* ▲▼ glyph; walls = `C`/`P`/`γ` letters on the rail, hollow = low-confidence retained; minimum 11 px type; drop italic-as-semantics.
7. **Closed-market mode** (medium / low): hide the 7 live-only columns, show session summary, demote score chips.
8. **Table grouping** (medium / medium): column groups Score | Volume | Flow | Walls | Clocks; frozen Symbol; default sort by rvol; `INLINE` rendered as plain text, only out-of-band gets a pill.
9. **Research → scorecard page** (medium / medium): put the scorecard first (hit rate by |S| × horizon with n and CI, calibration curve, conditioner lift), then the studies table, then a promotion-ladder status (steps 5–8 NOT BUILT).
10. **Replay/scrub** (medium / high): scrub bar over the last N minutes of one ticker from the 15 s feature table, overlaid with the decision log.
11. **Mobile watch mode** (low / medium): ≤8 names (the Rose seat cap), one line each: symbol · rvol · held score · wall distance · age.

**Execution-cockpit hierarchy.** Row 0 status bar: session · feed ages (Alpaca stream, FlashAlpha quota %, UW age) · risk budget (seats, open debit vs $5k, day P&L vs loss limit) · mode PAPER/LIVE. Zone A (left ~60%): Now lane + compact heatmap showing only names with rvol ≥1.5× or a held score, remainder collapsed to the sector strip. Zone B (right ~40%): focus panel + decision ticket. Zone C (bottom): positions with mark, MFE/MAE, bracket state, trigger chips; decision-log tail. Zone D (collapsible): news (time-sorted, score-filtered), momentum tape, macro.

**Research/scorecard hierarchy.** (1) Today's shadow scorecard vs 10-day; (2) calibration by score bucket with n; (3) conditioner lifts (rvol ≥3×, held 30 s / 5 m, momentum state); (4) studies table as today; (5) promotion ladder; (6) data coverage and gap audit (Field Guide › 13).

## (e) Turning the UI into a labeled-data capture device

- **One-click decisions on every tile/focus panel**: Call / Put / Pass / Watch. Each writes `{ts, ticker, actor (Jeffrey|Rose|Hal|shadow), action, snapshot_id (15 s score_features row), score+config version, rvol, C/P + coverage %, wall distances, lean + age, news tone, lane it appeared in, reason chips (surge | wall | flow | news | gut | sector), confidence 1–3, planned bracket}`. **Pass/Watch capture the negatives** the gap report says do not exist.
- **Automatic outcome labels** from the nightly `score_labels` job (5 m / 15 m / close forward returns already built) joined on `snapshot_id`; bracket counterfactuals (would +10%/+20%/stop have hit) computed from 5 s bars so manual exits and policy exits are comparable.
- **Forced exit tagging at close time**: reuse the 7 existing tags but require one at exit (not later) and keep administrative closes (`jeffrey_clear_rotate`, `USER_REQUESTED_FLAT`, `user_ordered_book_tp_harvest` per `DATA_GAP_REPORT.md`) in a separate category.
- **Alert acknowledgment as a label**: every surge/momentum/news alert gets Ack / Ignore / Acted; ignored alerts with strong forward returns become "missed" rows; false alerts become precision labels for the detector.
- **Structured Why**: replace the 69–543-char free text with reason chips + optional note; keep the note for case studies.
- **Dwell telemetry**: log which tile held hover/focus >2 s before a decision (attention label; cheap).
- **News corrections**: a thumbs-up/down on each finbert score (the visible +2 on a bearish post is exactly the training signal needed).
- **Extend the local review form** (`public/app.js` `inspectRecord`: status + note) with a rubric (entry quality, exit quality, process adherence) so post-hoc review and decision-time capture share one id.
- **Storage/leakage**: append rows to the mini's registry with `feature_available_at ≤ decision_at` and labels only in the evaluator (program.md knowledge-time rules); `oideval` holdout stays sealed.

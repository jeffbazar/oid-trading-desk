FIVE-BOT DATA SOURCE AND OPERATING PLAN
Prepared October 4, 2026. Recommended specification; no subscriptions, scheduling, bot runtime or website integration has been installed.

HOW TO USE THIS KIT
Each of 01–05 is a standalone operating prompt: role, sources, intervals, data fields, decision/alert rules and delivery requirements. The Rose/Hal prompts retain the detailed trade-review improvements. source-registry.json is the machine-readable catalog of 27 feed families. record-field-catalog.json specifies the shared envelope and proposed payload fields; it is not a full executable JSON Schema.

Use one shared collection/storage service and five analytical roles. A fast collector and deterministic position-risk monitor run independently from slower reasoning. API retrieval recovers complete records, push wakes a supported recipient, and the shared site presents the same committed state. Do not create five competing broker/news connections.

Customer/Supplier Event Map belongs inside News Alerts as a distinct module in this exactly-five-bot plan. Its relationship/project records and periodic graph maintenance remain separate from the fast headline path. If it later becomes a separate service, reuse these records and evidence, not another competing news collector.

BOT ASSIGNMENTS

ROSE TRADER
Owns short-term contract recommendations and position management. Primary: live equities/OPRA exact-contract prices, FA walls, UW flow, option IV/Greeks, ledger/risk and product reference data. Consumes Chart 5/15 min, Macro broad/tech judgments, verified News and the event calendar.
Collect held quotes by stream or 15–30 sec fallback; critical 5–10 sec where genuinely supported. Reason after each completed 5 min bar or material change; broader ranking 15 min. Entry/exit recommendations use new exact quotes. Watch stalled setups, giveback below the 50% trail arm, named failures, expiry and duplicated Hal exposure.

HAL TRADER
Owns durable thesis, valuation, catalyst timing, suitable duration and campaign outcomes. Primary: issuer/SEC actuals, dated current/PIT consensus, guidance/revisions, customer/supplier exposure, exact options and risk. Consumes Chart daily/weekly, Macro regime/shock and primary News.
Underlying safety stream/about 60 sec; option valuation 5 min, Greeks/position review 15 min. Deep premarket/midday/preclose and weekly thesis review, immediate material-event/binding-failure review. Tactical exceptions receive faster coverage. Contract remaining DTE, catalyst date and intended holding period are different fields.

MACRO BOT
Owns canonical economic-release actuals/revisions and separate Daily Macro / Macro Tech judgments. Sources: BLS/BEA/Census/Fed/EIA; frozen Trading Economics economist consensus; genuinely live yields, CME NQ/ES/fed-funds pricing, CME WTI/ICE Brent, VIX/dollar; FRED/ALFRED dated history and daily credit spreads.
Shared market stream or 30–60 sec; event-window 15–30 sec where supported. Release-driven interpretation and available 1/5/15 min transmission follow-ups; 15 min material-change checks, premarket/noon/preclose briefs. Seven input scores are evidence, never arithmetic addends. Tone labels are not a fixed lookup table.

CHART BOT
Owns numerical indicators/pattern states and validated forecasts only. Sources: shared numerical OHLCV/quotes, corporate actions, security master, sessions/calendar and benchmarks such as QQQ/SMH or SOXX.
Ingest 1 min/stream; evaluate completed 5/15/60 min; daily/weekly after actual completion and correction handling. New 1 min prices can monitor risk without pretending a 5 min pattern completed. No predictive probability until a registered calibrated model exists.

NEWS ALERTS BOT
Owns normalized company/policy/news claims, novelty, insider-filings interpretation and customer/supplier mapping. Sources: SEC filings, issuer IR, Benzinga news/earnings/ratings/transcripts, entitled allowlisted X discovery, official policy/regulatory statements and evidence-backed company relationships.
Persistent streams preferred; SEC 60–120 sec prioritized polling, IR 2–5 min ordinary/30–60 sec confirmed-release windows when allowed. Material minimum evidence alerts promptly, enrichment follows. Graph updates on evidence, daily active-edge checks and weekly broader audit. X fallback must disclose its limited coverage; existing Hal 2h scan is background until an authorized migration.

WHAT TO ADD FIRST
1. Verify live equity and exact-contract bid/ask/size/source time; archive quotes and fix signed paper accounting. Retain current RH path only where measured quality suffices.
2. Establish one numerical market-history/reference foundation: daily/intraday bars, splits/dividends, symbol/contract history and trading calendars.
3. Add push primary/news/event delivery and a current confirmed calendar; social polling alone does not protect opening-session news.
4. Add frozen economic consensus and canonical releases, then genuinely live macro instruments with explicit identities.
5. Enrich contract analysis with IV/Greeks and Hal with dated estimates/revisions/valuation. Current/PIT product timing differs.
6. Add the customer/supplier evidence module and evaluate patterns/option outcomes prospectively.

Sensible candidate stack: existing FA/UW; one entitled Databento or Massive price/history provider; ORATS as optional analytics enrichment; Benzinga push; official SEC/issuer/government sources; Trading Economics economic consensus; one licensed company-estimates product if needed. Provider names are alternatives or enrichment, not a requirement to buy every product. No exact vendor-price or plan-access claim is made.

PAYLOADS AND SHARING
Use immutable revisions in PostgreSQL (entities/instruments, documents, observations, setups, releases, assessments, relationships, decisions, positions, subscriptions, alerts/outbox and receipts). Keep raw time-series/history in partitioned storage/Parquet and permitted source evidence in object storage. An additional graph database is optional, not required for the initial map.

Minimum source metadata: feed/provider/instrument, source event/publication time, receipt/availability/evaluation time, units/basis, session/delay, adjustment/vintage, source revision and rights/lineage. Unknowns null; money decimals as strings. Quote last/mid/model/bid/ask are distinct. Actual underlying quotes stay separate from parity-derived ORATS stockPrice. Daily OI and daily FRED observations retain their own ages.

Source authority, confirmed disclosure, allocated economic exposure, expected stock direction, materiality, urgency and predictive confidence are different fields. A verified commitment is not delivered capacity/revenue. A chart fit is not a probability of a call profit.

Producers:
- Chart: chart_setup.
- Macro: economic_release, macro_snapshot.
- News: news_event, event_assessment, company_relationship and project_milestone.
- Rose/Hal: trader_decision; authorized ledger service handles position_revision.
- Infrastructure: quotes/bars/analytics/reference/health and recipient_alert delivery records.
Earlier shared-bot-records.schema.json covers supplier/event families only. Extend and validate each additional payload; do not claim this field catalog supplies full payload validation.

Proposed read API: consistent bootstrap+cursor, committed changes replay, exact record ID/revision lookup, combined context preserving each input's age, and source/coverage health. Producer writes are scoped by record family. Consumer acknowledgments mean durable receipt, not trade acceptance. Maintain existing shared-bot-api-contract recovery semantics; order by committed publication so late commits cannot be skipped.

Alert outbox commits atomically with records. P0 is an urgent held-thesis/risk/expiry/critical-coverage issue; P1 material new evidence or setup/target needing attention; P2 scheduled context. Corrected/retracted evidence reaches prior recipients. Keep unchanged runs quiet and deduplicate. A credible provisional high-severity report may warrant urgent risk review without being called confirmed.

Retention defaults are research proposals subject to storage/rights: 7 days readily queryable raw ticks, 90 days readily queryable candidate quote history, at least 2 years intraday bars where available, 5 years daily/history/corporate-action versions, and durable thesis/decision/evidence history. The 90-day window is a hot-storage target, not a deletion rule. Preserve each held campaign's licensed quote history and coverage records through its full life plus the chosen evaluation period, including multi-quarter Hal campaigns. Retain decision/entry/exit quote evidence with durable campaign records; use permitted derived evidence if raw-data rights expire, and disclose the resulting evaluation limits. Prefer full purchased historical retention where licensed. Track missing ranges; sampled quote history does not establish exact peaks or realistic fills.

HEALTH AND ACCEPTANCE
For each feed record expected session, coverage-through, actual collection lag, processing/delivery lag, missed ranges, source age, heartbeat, quota/entitlement and fallback status. A quiet stream is healthy if its heartbeat/coverage is intact.
Before calling a bot operational, demonstrate source availability, correct units/identities, calendar/split handling, source timestamps, replay/correction deduplication, stale-data blocks, role permissions, data-to-alert-to-receipt tracing and relevant paper accounting. Pattern/model validation is a separate stage. Test chronological outcomes with realistic cost/latency; no universal profit improvement is established.
